If you fast at the same times every day, the automatic schedule saves you from opening the app twice a day. It is part of **FastFast Pro**.

## Set up your schedule

1. Open **Settings → Fasting schedule** and turn on **Auto schedule**.
2. Choose a **plan**: 16:8, 18:6, 23:1 (one meal a day) or 36:12 (a 36-hour fast every other day).
3. Choose the **start time** – usually right after your last meal, for example 8:00 pm.
4. For 36:12, choose whether the **next start** is today or tomorrow.

FastFast schedules the next start notifications on your phone. No internet or account is needed.

## What happens each day

**At the start time** you get a notification with two buttons:

- **Start from schedule** – starts the fast at the scheduled time (for example 8:00 pm), even if you tap it a little later.
- **Start now** – starts the fast at the moment you tap.

**When you reach your goal** the notification includes an **End** button. Tapping it ends the fast at that moment. Next time you open the app you'll see the result screen with your Habit and shields.

You don't have to open the app. Your home-screen widget updates straight away.

### If you tap the notification instead of a button

FastFast opens and asks for the start time, pre-filled with the scheduled time, so you can adjust it to when you really stopped eating.

### If you didn't tap anything

Nothing is started automatically – a fast only counts when you start it. If you open the app later and tap **Start fasting**, FastFast offers the scheduled time as the start, so a late tap doesn't cost you hours.

> Why no fully automatic fasts? A schedule that runs by itself would build streaks you never actually fasted. FastFast always needs one tap from you – at least that tap is real.

## Notification buttons not working?

Some phones close apps in the background very aggressively. On those phones a button tap may not reach FastFast while it is closed. Nothing is lost: when you next open the app, it asks you to confirm the start time.

To make buttons and notifications more reliable, allow FastFast to run in the background. Menu names vary by phone and Android version:

| Phone | What to change |
|---|---|
| Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO | Settings → Apps → FastFast → **Autostart: on**, Battery saver → **No restrictions** |
| vivo / iQOO | Settings → Battery → Background power consumption → FastFast → **Allow** |
| OPPO / realme / OnePlus | Settings → Apps → FastFast → Battery usage → **Allow background activity** and **Allow auto launch** |
| Samsung | Settings → Apps → FastFast → Battery → **Unrestricted**; remove FastFast from **Sleeping apps** |
| Other Android | Settings → Apps → FastFast → Battery → **Unrestricted** / **Don't optimise** |

Also check that notifications for FastFast are allowed in Settings → Notifications. The site [dontkillmyapp.com](https://dontkillmyapp.com) has detailed, up-to-date steps for each brand.

## Tips

- Keep the schedule realistic. If your evenings vary, choose a start time you can hit most days, and use **Start now** on the others.
- The schedule only sends notifications for the next few starts each time you open the app. If you don't open FastFast for a week, it stops nudging you – no endless reminders.
- Changed your mind? Turn the schedule off at any time; your history stays as it is.
