FastFast is built to stay out of your way: one big button, a clear timer, and everything else one tap away. This guide walks through each part.

## 1. Choose your goal

Tap the goal on the home screen to pick how long you want to fast. Each goal comes with a short explanation and advice:

| Goal | Hours | Level |
|---|---|---|
| Intermittent | 16 h | Beginner |
| Fat Burner | 18 h | Beginner+ |
| Warrior | 20 h | Intermediate |
| OMAD | 23 h | Intermediate |
| Monk | 36 h | Advanced |
| Extended | 48 h | Advanced |
| Prolonged | 72 h | Expert |

New to fasting? Start with 16 hours or less – see [your first week of 16:8](first-week-16-8.html).

## 2. Start and end a fast

- Tap **Start fasting** right after your last meal.
- Tap **End** when you eat again. FastFast shows a result screen with your fasting time, progress toward the goal, Habit and shields.
- **Forgot to tap?** Hold the button instead of tapping it. You can pick an earlier start time or the exact end time, including on another day.

The circle on the home screen fills as you fast and changes colour as you pass each goal. Below it, **Body phases** show what is likely happening in your body – fat burning, ketosis and more. Tap the **?** icon for an explanation, or read [what happens hour by hour](body-timeline.html).

## 3. Log your day

Next to the main button you'll find quick actions for today:

- **Mood** – five levels from very bad to very good, with an optional note
- **Weight** – log it whenever you like; trends appear in statistics
- **Photo** – one photo per day, from the camera or your gallery

Photos and notes stay on your phone (and in your own backup if you turn it on).

## 4. Track your progress

The **Statistics** tab shows your current and best streak, total fasting hours, completed fasts, average and longest fast, active days and shields used, with charts over weeks and months.

**Achievements** unlock as you go – total fasting hours, long fasts, streaks and more – in bronze, silver, gold and diamond tiers. You can pick favourite badges to show off in your Journey image.

## 5. Your year in pixels

The **Journey** tab turns every day of the year into a coloured cell – by mood or by fasting hours. It is the best way to see your consistency at a glance. See [Journey calendar and sharing](journey-calendar.html).

## 6. Home-screen widgets (Android)

Long-press your home screen → **Widgets** → **FastFast**. There are two:

- **FastFast** – fasting progress with a start/stop button
- **FastFast mini** – a compact timer with a start/stop button

The widget updates when your fast changes. Tapping start or stop opens the app to confirm, which keeps it reliable on phones that restrict background apps.

## 7. Notifications

In **Settings → Notifications**:

- **Target reached** – a notification when you hit your goal
- **Daily reminder** – a gentle nudge at a time you choose

With FastFast Pro you can also set an [automatic fasting schedule](auto-schedule.html) with Start and End buttons right on the notification.

## 8. Make it yours

In **Settings** you can switch light/dark mode, themes, decorative effects and their intensity, icon packs, language (10 languages), which day the week starts on, sounds and vibration, and turn beginner tips on or off. Some themes, effects and icon packs are part of Pro; seasonal themes are free during their event (the Christmas theme is free from December 1 to 31).

## 9. Learn as you go

**Settings → Fasting guide** contains short, sourced articles you can read offline. Links also appear where you need them – for example, what breaks a fast while you are fasting, and how to eat again after a long fast.

Questions? Use **Settings → Feedback** or see the [FAQ](faq.html).
