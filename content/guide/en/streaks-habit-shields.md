FastFast rewards consistency, not extremes. Four numbers work together: your **streak**, your **Habit** bar, **Retain** and **shields**.

## Habit – how strong your routine is

Habit is a bar from 0% to 100% that grows with every qualifying fast.

- Each fast of **16 hours or more** adds **+3%**.
- Every hour beyond 16 adds another **+0.2%** – a 20-hour fast gives +3.8%.
- Fasts shorter than 16 hours don't add to Habit.

So two or three solid fasts a week keep the bar climbing; there is no need to push extreme hours.

## Streak – days in a row

Your streak counts consecutive days of your fasting routine. It is shown on the home screen and in statistics, together with your best streak ever.

## Shields – rest days without losing progress

Life happens. Shields let you take a day off without breaking your streak.

- Each **empty day between two fasts** uses **1 shield**. Your streak stays valid and your Habit is unchanged.
- If you rest **with no shields left**, the streak breaks, Habit drops (the longer the gap, the more it drops) and Retain resets to 0.
- You can hold **up to 3 shields**. Extra shields beyond that are lost.

### How to earn shields

| How | Shields |
|---|---|
| A fast of 48 hours or more | +1 |
| A fast of 72 hours or more | +2 |
| Habit reaching 35%, 70% and 100% | +1 at each milestone |
| Every 25 Retain points | +1 |

Long fasts give shields on purpose: after 48–72 hours your body needs a day or two of normal eating to recover (see [breaking a long fast](breaking-a-long-fast.html)). Shields cover those days.

If your Habit drops and climbs back past a milestone, you can earn that milestone shield again.

## Retain – after you hit 100%

Once Habit is at 100%, the points from each fast go into **Retain** instead. When Retain reaches **25**, it automatically turns into one shield. It is a reward for keeping a strong routine over time.

## Why it works this way

- **No punishment for rest.** Rest days, social dinners and sick days are normal. Shields make them part of the plan.
- **No reward for danger.** Habit grows a little faster for longer fasts, but the biggest gains come from regularity, not from pushing to 72 hours.
- **Honest data.** Fasts only count when you start and end them – there are no automatic sessions that run without you.

> If you feel unwell, end your fast. A shorter day is always better than a streak at any cost. See [who should not fast](who-should-not-fast.html).

## Where to see it

- **Home:** current streak and Habit
- **Result screen:** Habit gained, shields earned and their source after each fast
- **Statistics:** current and best streak, shields used
- **Journey:** days protected by a shield show a shield icon
