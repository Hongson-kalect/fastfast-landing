FastFast works fully offline. Your fasts, notes, moods, weight and photos are stored **only on your phone**. We don't run a server that receives your data, and you don't need an account.

That also means: if you lose your phone or uninstall the app without a backup, your data is gone. Backups fix that.

## Back up to Google Drive

1. Open **Settings → Backup & Restore**.
2. Tap **Sign in with Google** and choose your account.
3. Tap **Back up now**. FastFast uploads your data and photos.

FastFast only asks for the `drive.appdata` permission. The backup goes into a **hidden app folder in your own Google Drive** – FastFast can't see or touch any of your other files, and the backup is never sent to us.

Each new backup replaces the previous one. The screen shows when your last backup was made.

## Back up to a file

Prefer not to use Google? In **Backup & Restore → File**, export a backup file and save it wherever you like – your phone's storage, a USB drive or another cloud service.

## Move to a new phone

1. On your old phone, make a fresh backup (Drive or file).
2. Install FastFast on the new phone.
3. Open **Settings → Backup & Restore** and choose **Restore from Drive** (signed in with the same Google account) or import your backup file.

> Restoring **replaces** all data on the current device with the backup. Make sure you restore the right one.

## Signing out

Signing out of Google keeps all data on your phone and keeps your backup on Drive. You can sign back in at any time.

## Deleting your data

- **On your phone:** uninstalling FastFast or clearing its storage deletes all local data. **Settings → Reset settings** resets preferences only.
- **On Google Drive:** open Google Drive on the web → Settings → **Manage apps** → FastFast → **Delete hidden app data**.

## What FastFast never does

- No ads and no advertising IDs
- No analytics or tracking SDKs
- No selling or sharing of your data
- No account required

The internet is only used for Google sign-in, Drive backup and restore, and purchases through Google Play. Read the full [privacy policy](../../privacy.html).
