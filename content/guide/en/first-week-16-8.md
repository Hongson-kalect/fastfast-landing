Jumping straight into 16 hours can feel rough. This plan builds up over a week so your body and your routine can adjust. Adjust the clock times to your own day – the idea is what matters.

## Before you start

- Choose a **fixed last meal time** you can keep most days, for example 8 pm.
- Plan two or three real meals for your eating window. Protein and vegetables keep you full longest.
- Keep water, black coffee or plain tea at hand for the morning.
- Read [what breaks a fast](what-breaks-a-fast.html) so you know what is allowed.

## The 7-day plan

| Day | Fast | Example | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 12 h | 8 pm → 8 am | Just stop evening snacks |
| 2 | 12 h | 8 pm → 8 am | Notice when hunger shows up |
| 3 | 13 h | 8 pm → 9 am | Water first thing in the morning |
| 4 | 14 h | 8 pm → 10 am | Coffee or tea to bridge the morning |
| 5 | 14 h | 8 pm → 10 am | Protein-rich first meal |
| 6 | 15 h | 8 pm → 11 am | Stay busy in the late morning |
| 7 | 16 h | 8 pm → 12 pm | Your first full 16:8 🎉 |

If a day feels too hard, repeat the previous level. Going slower is not failing.

## Handling hunger

- **Hunger comes in waves.** It usually peaks around your old breakfast time and fades within 20–30 minutes.
- **Drink something.** A glass of water or sparkling water often takes the edge off.
- **Stay busy.** Hunger is louder when you are bored.
- **Eat enough in your window.** Under-eating the day before makes the next fast harder.
- **Sleep.** Short sleep raises appetite the next day.

## Your first meal

Break the fast with a normal, balanced meal – not a sugar rush. Something like eggs and vegetables, rice with fish and greens, or yogurt with fruit and nuts. Eat slowly; your hunger signals need a few minutes to catch up.

## Common mistakes

1. **Making up for it** with very large, sugary meals in the window.
2. **Not drinking enough**, then blaming fasting for headaches.
3. **Too much too soon** – jumping to 20 hours on day two.
4. **Ignoring sleep and stress**, which make cravings much stronger.
5. **All-or-nothing thinking.** A 14-hour day is still a good day.

## Using FastFast for your first week

- Tap **Start fasting** after your last meal. Forgot? Hold the button to pick an earlier start time.
- Set your goal (start with **Intermittent – 16h** on day 7) and turn on the target-reached notification.
- Log your mood and weight in the evening; after a week you will see how you actually feel, not how you think you feel.
