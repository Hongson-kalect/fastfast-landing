Journey shows a whole year on one screen. Each day is a small cell, coloured by how you felt or how long you fasted. After a few weeks it becomes the most honest picture of your routine.

## Two modes

### Mood

Each day you log a mood (from very bad to very good), its cell takes that colour or icon. Days with a written note get a border in your theme colour. Over time you can see whether good and bad days cluster – around long fasts, busy weeks or poor sleep.

### Fasting

Each cell is coloured by the **total length of the longest fast that touches that day**. A fast that spans several days colours all of them – lighter on the first day and darker toward the end. A **shield icon** marks a rest day protected by a shield.

**Heatmap** (Pro) switches to a different view: each cell shows how many hours you fasted on that calendar day, the darker the more.

## Reading the legend

Under the calendar, the legend shows each level with the number of days in that level this year – for example how many days were 16 h+ fasts or "good" moods.

## Actions

- **Tap an empty day** to add a mood and note.
- **Tap a day with data** to see details: fasts, mood, note, weight and photo.
- Use the **Week / Day** button in the corner of the grid to switch between a week-based and a month-based layout, vertical or horizontal.

## Export and share your year

1. Tap **Save image** in Journey.
2. A preview opens with the image in your current layout and theme, including your year statistics and – if you chose them – your favourite badges.
3. Choose **Save image** to put it in your gallery, or **Share** to send it straight to messaging apps or social media.

Choose which badges appear in **Settings → Showcase**, and turn them off for the export if you prefer a clean calendar.

## Share a single fast

After ending a fast, the result screen has a **Share** button. It creates a card in your current theme with your fasting time, goal and progress, and your streak – sized for feeds and stories.

> Everything is generated on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share it.
