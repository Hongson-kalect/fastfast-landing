## The app

### Is FastFast free?

Yes. The timer, all fasting goals, streaks, shields, Habit, notes, mood, weight, photos, statistics, achievements, the Journey calendar, widgets, backups and the fasting guide are free, with no ads.

### What does FastFast Pro add?

Pro is about personalisation and convenience: premium colour themes, decorative effects, mood and goal icon packs, the Journey heatmap and the [automatic fasting schedule](auto-schedule.html). Buying Pro also supports an independent app without ads. Purchases go through Google Play.

### Are seasonal themes free?

Seasonal themes are free during their event. The Christmas theme, with its effects and icon packs, is free from **December 1 to December 31** every year.

### Is there an iPhone version?

FastFast is launching on Android first. An iOS version is planned.

### Which languages are supported?

English, Vietnamese, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Indonesian.

## Privacy and data

### Do I need an account?

No. You only sign in with Google if you want to back up to your own Google Drive.

### Does FastFast work offline?

Yes, completely. The internet is only used for Google sign-in, Drive backup and restore, and Google Play purchases.

### Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Optional backups go to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive or to a file you export. See [backup and privacy](backup-and-privacy.html).

### Do you track me or show ads?

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Read the [privacy policy](../../privacy.html).

## Fasting

### Which goal should I start with?

Start with 12–14 hours and move up to 16 hours (**Intermittent**) over a week or two. See [your first week of 16:8](first-week-16-8.html).

### I forgot to start or end my fast. Can I fix it?

Yes. Hold the main button instead of tapping it to pick the exact start or end time.

### Why didn't my short fast count?

Fasts shorter than 16 hours don't add to your Habit bar. They are still saved in your history. See [streaks, Habit and shields](streaks-habit-shields.html).

### Are the body phases on the timer accurate?

They are research-based estimates, not measurements. Earlier phases like fat burning and ketosis are well documented; later ones such as autophagy and immune renewal are based mostly on animal studies. See [what happens hour by hour](body-timeline.html).

### Is fasting safe for me?

For most healthy adults, daily 12–16 hour fasts are safe. Some people should not fast without medical supervision – read [who should not fast](who-should-not-fast.html).

## Troubleshooting

### I don't get notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed for FastFast in your phone settings, and that the app is allowed to run in the background. See the brand-by-brand table in [auto schedule](auto-schedule.html#notification-buttons-not-working).

### The widget doesn't update.

Remove and add the widget again, open FastFast once, and allow it to run in the background on phones with aggressive battery saving.

### How do I contact you?

Use **Settings → Feedback** in the app – it opens an email with your app version and device info filled in – or write to [fastfast.app.support@gmail.com](mailto:fastfast.app.support@gmail.com).
